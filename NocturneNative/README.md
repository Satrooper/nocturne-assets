# Nocturne: Black Rain — v6 (passes 1–3)

A native Godot 4.5.2 third-person action game for iPhone. Five stage arenas,
seven firearms, a greatsword combo, a jet-boost dodge and an original soundtrack.
The ZIP is source code; build the IPA with your existing GitHub workflow.

## v6 passes 2–3

**Your face (player).** A sculpted head built from your photos: sampled skin tone,
voluminous curly hair (lumpy volume + frizzy outer layer), full beard joined to a
moustache, thick brows, brown eyes, ears. It replaces the soldier's helmet and follows
the head bone's animation. Stylised, not a scan — the honest limit of building a face
in code from photos. (`scripts/head.gd`, `scripts/head_rig.gd`)

**A different enemy faction per stage** (`scripts/costume.gd`), each with human faces
of varied skin tones, hair and beards underneath, distinct headgear, outfits, stance
and walking pace:
Neon Underworld — Syndicate (hoods/caps, LED bandanas) · Glass Palace — Security
(suits, ties, shades, earpieces) · Hollow Bamboo — Shinobi (kasa hats, face wraps,
low stance, faster gait) · Ashworks — Furnace crew (hard hats, gas masks, glowing
goggles, air tanks) · Black Cathedral — Cult (porcelain masks, pointed hoods, robes,
swaying walk). New attack animations would need motion-capture files.

**Expanded multi-room maps** (`cathedral_depths`, `bamboo_grounds` in `scripts/sets.gd`).
Each wave spawns one room deeper:
- Black Cathedral: nave → chancel (raised altar stage, pipe organ, rose window,
  chandeliers, choir stalls, banners, paintings) → library (walls of books, reading
  tables, globe, ladder) → crypt boss arena (colonnade, tombs, cloaked statues, bone
  niches, candle fields, light shaft).
- Hollow Bamboo: grove → torii gate → shrine courtyard (stone lanterns, koi pond with
  lilies and lotus, cherry trees with falling petals, shrine and bell) → teahouse
  (tatami, lit shoji walls, low tables, tea sets, lanterns, ink scrolls) → flower
  meadow boss arena (dense grass, wildflowers, waterfall, mist, fireflies).
- Original paintings and scrolls generated by `tools/make_art.py`.

**Performance for bigger maps.** Geometry is batched per material per room (each room
gets its own nearest 8 lamps, the phone renderer's per-mesh limit); large walls carry
occluders and occlusion culling is on, so rooms behind walls are not drawn; grass and
props fade out with distance. Navigation scales to any map size.

Not yet expanded: Neon Underworld, Glass Palace, Ashworks (next pass).

Tests: 174/174 (`tests/integration.gd`), including a full playthrough of every stage
and a check that each boss room is reachable on foot.

## v6 pass 1

- **Pause now freezes everything** (animations, enemies, particles, tweens). v5 only
  stopped input, so the character kept playing its run cycle.
- **Laser fixed.** Beam and bullet tracers were scaled along the world's vertical
  axis instead of along the shot, so the beam stayed ~1 m long. It now reaches the
  hit point: white-hot core, cyan glow sheath, muzzle flare and impact bloom.
- **Weapon wheel.** Tap ⇄ to cycle your three guns; hold ⇄ to open a wheel with all
  seven (time slows while it is open). Picking an uncarried gun replaces the current
  slot. Desktop: Tab opens it, 1–7 equip directly. ARSENAL is also in the pause menu.
- **Faster sword.** Light cuts 0.40 s → 0.25 s, slam 0.74 s → 0.46 s; chains as soon
  as each strike lands, more damage, and hits interrupt normal enemies' attacks.
- **Jump.** JUMP button (Space on desktop). Press again in the air for a jet lift,
  or BOOST in the air for an air-dash. Landing thud and camera kick.
- **Realistic gunfire.** Layered synthesis per shot: firing-pin click, supersonic
  crack, saturated muzzle blast, low thump, receiver resonance, bolt cycling, early
  reflections and rumble tail; three variants per gun picked at random. Each stage
  adds its own room acoustics (street slap, palace hall, open bamboo, refinery,
  cathedral echo) on gunfire and explosions.
- **Footsteps** for hard floors, grass and metal grating (4 variants each), plus jump
  and landing sounds.
- **AUDIO mix**: separate volume for Master, Music, Guns & Sword, Explosions & Hits,
  Footsteps & Jets, Menu clicks.
- **RADIO** with 12 tracks (5 new): AUTO follows stage and boss music, or lock any
  station. Tap ♫ in battle to skip. Your own files still override any track via
  `assets/music/custom/`.

Tests: 168/168 (`tests/integration.gd`).

## What changed in v5

**Everything unlocked.** SELECT CONTRACT starts any of the five stages directly.
Cleared stages get a ✓.

**Performance / heat (the main fix).** v4 sent 900–1,750 draw calls per frame;
the level geometry was split into up to 275 tiny batches, each redrawn into four
shadow cascades, and 3D rendered at full native resolution through OpenGL.
- Renderer: Metal ("Mobile") instead of OpenGL Compatibility.
- 3D renders at 77% (Balanced) and is upscaled with Apple MetalFX; HUD stays native.
- Level geometry merged per material: 17–51 batches per stage (was 115–275).
- Shadows: 2 cascades (was 4); small trim, armour and weapon parts no longer cast
  shadows. Shadow casters in stage 1: 49 (was 293).
- Idle muzzle lights hidden; bullets/sparks/tracers pooled instead of created per
  shot; aim-assist checks cached; cheaper enemy IK; distant lamps fade out.
- Physics interpolation for smooth motion at 60 or 120 fps.
- 60 fps default (cooler, smoother). 120 fps ProMotion option in GRAPHICS —
  applies after fully closing and reopening the app.
- Adaptive resolution steps 3D resolution down briefly if the phone throttles.
- Glow/bloom added on Balanced and Quality for the neon and emissive weapons.

**Weapons (ARSENAL: carry any 3, SWAP cycles).**
V-9 sidearm · R-41 assault rifle · Breach shotgun · Ravager minigun (spinning
barrels, 20 rounds/s) · Hellfire rocket launcher (area explosions) · Arc beam
laser (continuous, vents when empty) · Quad star blaster (twin plasma bolts that
curve onto targets).

**Greatsword.** Tap SWORD up to three times: two fast cuts, then a leaping
overhead slam with a shockwave. Glowing runed blade, slash trails, hit-stop,
screen shake, soft lock-on to the nearest enemy, torso twist into each swing.

**Boost dodge.** Jet-dash in the stick direction with boot and back thrusters,
exhaust trail, forward lean and lift; briefly invulnerable.

**Audio.** Seven original looping tracks (menu, one per stage, boss) with
crossfades; boss music starts when the boss spawns. 19 new effects (per-weapon
shots, explosion, laser hum, sword swings/impacts, slam, boost, UI). All audio
is generated by `tools/make_audio.py` (no third-party audio). To use your own
tracks, see `assets/music/custom/README.txt`.

**Touch fix (from earlier).** Taps reach the menu buttons on iPhone.

## Campaign

| Stage | Environment | Interaction | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neon Underworld | Rain, railway steelwork, shopfronts, parked cars, wet asphalt | Disable relay | Enforcer: shotgun spreads and charge |
| Glass Palace | Marble lobby, chandeliers, reception, aquarium displays, sofas | Override security | Director: strafing fire, marked blasts, reinforcements |
| Hollow Bamboo | Bamboo grove, ferns, shrine gates, lanterns, side stream | Ring bell | Bellkeeper: charges and delayed ground strikes |
| Ashworks | Rusted refinery, tanks, pipes, catwalks, furnace | Close valve | Furnace: delayed fire zones and vulnerable recovery |
| Black Cathedral | Stone arches, stained glass, pews, candles, snow | Break seal | Warden: ranged spreads, charges and second-phase blast |

Each level is a compact combat arena. Clear waves, reach the marked interaction,
then survive the final wave and boss. The first four
have three waves; the last has four. There are 108 scheduled regular enemies,
five bosses, and two additional guards summoned by the Director's second phase.
Enemies are limited to small active groups, not all spawned simultaneously.

## Run on Windows

1. Install Godot **4.5.2 Standard**, not .NET.
2. Extract this ZIP, import `NocturneNative/project.godot`, and allow asset import.
3. Press F5, then select NEW CONTRACT. No export templates are needed for this.

## Validation and limits

`docs/TEST_RESULTS.txt` records 98 passing integration checks. Actual desktop
Godot captures are in `docs/previews/`; these are not generated artwork. Boss
captures use staged positions and invulnerability to keep the subjects visible.

This is still a procedural-art prototype, below the reference games' AAA quality.
Characters share a skeletal model; bosses do not have bespoke motion-captured
attack animations. There are no vehicles to drive, cinematic story scenes,
advanced destruction, ragdolls, or open-world exploration. Upper walkways and
backdrop buildings are scenery, not separate playable floors.

iPhone frame rate, touch ergonomics, signing, installation, battery use and heat
have not been tested. Instancing and capped effects are present, but render cost
is substantial and further mobile profiling/optimization is needed. Desktop
software-renderer captures do not establish phone performance.
